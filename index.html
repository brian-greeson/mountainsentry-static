Product Brand

ShotSense

ShotSense is Mountain Sentry's own connected product, showing the same hardware, mobile, BLE, and sensor experience founders often need for their first prototype.

What it is

ShotSense is a compact motion capture device that mounts to the end of a pickleball paddle handle and records motion during play with an onboard IMU.

The device streams motion data to an iOS app over Bluetooth Low Energy so players can see objective feedback instead of relying only on feel.

What players learn

  • Paddle orientation
  • Swing timing and repeatability
  • Swing consistency and other motion characteristics
  • Real-time feedback and post-session analysis

Product goal

Provide pickleball players with simple, objective feedback about swing mechanics so they can train more effectively and improve faster.

Battery life, low power operation, and measurable training feedback are central to the product design.
