# Founder Prototype Positioning Design

## Goal

Refocus the Mountain Sentry homepage toward founders and early-stage startups who need help turning an idea, partial app, or connected product concept into a working prototype.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Solo founders with a product idea but no full engineering team
- Early-stage startups with a partial app or rough proof of concept
- Founders building products where hardware and software meet
- Teams that need a practical builder to move from uncertainty to a testable prototype

Secondary audience:

- Product teams that need focused contract engineering support
- Visitors evaluating Mountain Sentry through ShotSense as a proof point

## Positioning

Mountain Sentry should lead as a founder prototype partner. The core promise is hands-on help taking product ideas from concept to initial working prototype, especially when the product involves mobile apps, connected hardware, Bluetooth Low Energy, sensors, embedded workflows, or other hardware/software boundaries.

ShotSense should remain visible, but it should support the main sales story as proof that Mountain Sentry can build real connected products.

## Homepage Copy Structure

### Hero

Lead with founder-specific product realization language:

- Headline direction: turn a connected product idea into a working prototype
- Supporting copy: speak to founders with an idea, a partial app, or uncertainty around the hardware/software edge
- Primary call to action: start a prototype conversation
- Secondary calls to action: view capabilities and see ShotSense

### About

Describe Mountain Sentry as a hands-on partner for founders and early-stage teams. Emphasize practical product judgment, early architecture, prototype delivery, and the ability to cross mobile, software, and device boundaries.

### Capabilities

Recast the capability cards around founder problems:

- Idea to Prototype
- Hardware + App Integration
- Mobile Product Development
- Startup Engineering Support

Each card should describe a concrete way Mountain Sentry helps reduce ambiguity and move the product closer to something users, investors, or partners can evaluate.

### ShotSense

Keep the ShotSense section, but frame it as evidence of connected product experience. It should show that Mountain Sentry has built a real app-plus-hardware product involving embedded hardware, BLE, motion sensing, and an iOS experience.

### Contact

Change the contact section from generic contract inquiry language to founder-oriented prompts:

- What are you trying to build?
- What exists today?
- Where are you stuck?
- What would an initial prototype need to prove?

## Constraints

- Keep the existing static site structure and visual layout.
- Avoid adding new pages or complex interactions.
- Keep copy concise enough for the current cards and responsive layout.
- Preserve ShotSense as a product brand on the page.
- Keep contact email and legal links unchanged.

## Success Criteria

- A founder can tell within the first viewport that Mountain Sentry helps turn product ideas into prototypes.
- The page speaks directly to solo founders and early-stage startup teams.
- Hardware/software integration reads as a differentiator, not a generic service bullet.
- ShotSense supports credibility without distracting from the services offer.
- The revised copy still fits the existing layout on desktop and mobile.
