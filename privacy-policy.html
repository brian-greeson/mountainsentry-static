Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 6, 2026

Mountain Sentry ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us about contract work, or use ShotSense and related mobile applications and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you contact us, create an account, or request support, we may collect your name, email, or other contact details you provide.
  • Automatically Collected Data: Device type, operating system, app version, usage data, crash reports, and analytics through Apple or third-party SDKs we use to operate and improve our software.
  • ShotSense Sensor and Session Data: When you use ShotSense, we may collect motion and session data generated by the paddle-mounted device and iOS app, such as inertial measurements, timestamps, derived swing metrics, and related training analysis.
  • Permissions: Depending on the features you use, we may request access to Bluetooth, notifications, or other device permissions needed to pair hardware, stream data, and support the app experience. You can manage or revoke these permissions in your iOS settings.

How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve our website, apps, and engineering services
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries about contract work or products
  • To connect ShotSense hardware to the iOS app and generate player feedback and session analysis
  • To analyze performance, fix bugs, and improve system reliability and battery life
  • To comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, apps, analytics, or support systems, or when required by law. All partners are contractually bound to protect your data.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely secure, but we work to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide our services, support product development and analytics, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Rights

You may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, withdraw consent, or opt out of marketing communications, depending on your location. To make a request, contact privacy@mountainsentry.com.

Children’s Privacy

Our apps and website are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in other countries. We use safeguards to ensure adequate protection of your personal information.

App Store Disclosure

Our iOS apps comply with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines and App Privacy requirements. You can review app-specific data collection details for ShotSense and any future app offerings in the App Store listing under “App Privacy.”

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will appear on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us:

  • Email: privacy@mountainsentry.com
  • Website: www.mountainsentry.com
