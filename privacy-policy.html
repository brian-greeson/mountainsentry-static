1. Scope

Mountain Sentry Corp ("Mountain Sentry," "we," "us," or "our") provides software, connected-device tools, websites, and engineering services. This policy explains how information is handled across those products and services and when you contact us. The information involved depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

2. Information we handle

Contact and business information. When you contact us or request services, we receive information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, and the details of your inquiry.

Communications and support content. We receive messages, correspondence, attachments, screenshots, diagnostic reports, configuration files, and other material that you choose to send us for support or project work. Please do not include passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information unless we have specifically arranged an appropriate way to receive it.

Technical and network request information. Our website hosting, email, form, and other service providers may process information necessary to deliver and secure their services, such as IP addresses, browser or operating-system information, request times, requested resources, and error or security logs. External content providers may receive similar technical information when a software feature requests their content.

Information processed on your device. Our software may use or store settings, preferences, files, designs, connected-device readings, configuration information, and other content on your device. Local processing does not by itself give Mountain Sentry access to that information. We do not automatically upload this app content to Mountain Sentry. You can choose to export, share, back up, or submit information using available features or operating-system controls.

Information used by optional network features and integrations. When you use a feature that requests external content or connects to another service, the information needed for that feature may leave your device. This can include selected map areas or tile coordinates, requested firmware or documentation, and technical request information. Optional integrations may also receive the connected-device, profile, configuration, diagnostic, or other content that you choose to make available to them. These transmissions are different from Mountain Sentry collecting and retaining the information on its own servers.

We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs in our apps. We do not automatically receive app usage histories or crash reports from our apps; you may choose to send diagnostics to support. Operating-system and app-store providers may process information under their own privacy settings and policies.

3. Sources and purposes

Information comes from you, your device and connected equipment, and the providers involved in the features or communications you use. We use information that we receive to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Provide requested software, engineering, and business services.
  • Investigate problems and improve reliability using information you submit.
  • Deliver website content and communications and protect services from abuse.
  • Maintain necessary business records and comply with legal obligations.

Information processed locally is used to provide the features you select. Information sent to an external provider is used to fulfill the associated request or integration.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on providing requested services or performing a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our business, your consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers. We use providers for website hosting, email, and contact-form delivery. Our website contact form uses Web3Forms to deliver the details you submit. Providers processing personal information on our behalf must use it only to provide the relevant services and must provide the same or an equal level of protection described in this policy. Web3Forms' privacy policy is available at https://web3forms.com/privacy.

External content and services. Features may request maps, imagery, firmware, documentation, or other content directly from third-party providers. Those providers receive the request and associated technical information and handle it under their own policies. A request sent directly to a provider does not mean Mountain Sentry receives or stores a copy.

Optional AI and other integrations. Some software features can connect to an independently operated AI service or another application. Access to local app information is disabled until you enable or authorize the integration. When enabled, the integration may receive information exposed through the feature, including connected-device status, profile information, configuration values, diagnostics, or pending changes. An AI service may transmit that information to its operator for processing. For example, a ChatGPT integration may send information to OpenAI for inference. The provider's privacy policy and your account settings govern its retention and other processing. Review the integration's explanation and provider settings before enabling it, avoid sharing sensitive information, and disable access when you no longer want to use it. Other features remain usable without enabling optional AI access.

At your direction. Information may be shared when you deliberately export or send it, select a destination, connect equipment, or use a third-party backup or sharing service.

Legal and safety needs. We may disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal process, protect rights and safety, prevent abuse, or resolve disputes.

5. Retention

We retain contact messages, support submissions, and business records only as long as needed for their stated purposes, ongoing support or business relationships, security, or applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of information and why it was provided. We delete information when it is no longer needed. Limited copies may remain in backups until their normal deletion cycle completes.

Local app data remains under your control. You can remove it using available app controls or by deleting the app and its associated data. Device, cloud, and computer backups must be managed separately through the relevant provider. Deleting an app does not necessarily delete exports, backups, support messages, or information already provided to an independent service.

6. Deletion requests and other privacy choices

To request deletion of personal information held by Mountain Sentry, email support@mountainsentry.com with the subject "Privacy deletion request." Include enough information to identify the messages, support request, or other records involved. Do not send passwords or identity documents with the initial request.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request is yours. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If we must retain information for a legal obligation, security, or another permitted exception, we will explain the applicable limitation. If we hold no relevant information, we will tell you. We cannot directly delete data held only on your device or in an independent provider's account; we can explain the available controls where appropriate.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision about a privacy request. Send these requests or appeals to support@mountainsentry.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

You can manage device permissions in operating-system settings. Declining or withdrawing a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but it does not remove information already stored or shared. Optional integrations can be disabled using their available access controls.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts, and our website code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Hosting and form providers may process technical information to deliver their services, prevent abuse, and maintain security. Our apps may use local storage for preferences and functionality; that storage is not used for cross-app advertising tracking.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for information we receive. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Protect your devices, accounts, exports, and backups, and share only information appropriate for the recipient.

Mountain Sentry is based in the United States. Information handled by us or the providers you use may be processed in the United States or other countries. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards must apply to international transfers.

9. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support@mountainsentry.com so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will post the updated policy and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent before making a material change to how personal information is used.
